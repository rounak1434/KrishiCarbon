            About KrishiCarbon

            KrishiCarbon is a readiness assessment platform designed to help Indian farmers understand how prepared they are to participate in carbon credit programs.

            Carbon credit programs incentivize sustainable farming practices that reduce greenhouse gas emissions and improve soil carbon sequestration. However, participating in these programs often requires specific practices, documentation, and records that many farmers may not be aware of.

            This platform bridges that gap by providing:

            • A structured assessment of your current farming practices
            • Identification of strengths and gaps in readiness
            • Practical, actionable recommendations
            • Guidance on what records and documentation you need

            What are Carbon Credits?

            Carbon credits are tradeable certificates representing the reduction or removal of one tonne of carbon dioxide (CO₂) equivalent from the atmosphere. Farmers can earn carbon credits by adopting practices that increase soil carbon, reduce emissions, or improve resource efficiency.

            Who is this for?

            This platform is designed for:

            • Individual farmers looking to understand carbon credit programs
            • Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) assessing group readiness
            • Agricultural extension workers helping farmers prepare
            • NGOs and development organizations working with farming communities

            Important Limitations

            ⚠️

            This platform is a prototype readiness assessment tool. It does not issue carbon credits, verify farm practices, guarantee eligibility for any program, or promise financial returns. Always consult with authorized agencies and carbon credit program administrators for actual enrollment.
