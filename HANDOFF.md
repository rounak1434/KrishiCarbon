## 6. Organic Fertilizer Management (Engine v1.1.0)

### 6.1 Database Schema
- `FertilizerCategory`: `ORGANIC`, `SYNTHETIC`, `INTEGRATED`
- `OrganicFertilizerType`: `COMPOST`, `FARMYARD_MANURE`, `VERMICOMPOST`, `BIOFERTILIZER`, `GREEN_MANURE`, `OTHER`
- `Farm` fields: `fertilizerCategory`, `organicFertilizerType`, `fertilizerUsage` (backwards-compatible default `"NOT_SPECIFIED"`).

### 6.2 Deterministic Scoring
- Organic: `+12 pts` Farming Practices, `+10 pts` Soil Management (+ sub-type bonuses: Vermicompost/Biofertilizer `+3`, Compost/FYM/Green Manure `+2`, Other `+1`).
- Integrated: `+7 pts` Farming Practices, `+6 pts` Soil Management.
- Excessive Synthetic: `-6 pts` Farming Practices, `-5 pts` Soil Management.
- Missing: `0 pts` neutral baseline (incomplete evidence, no invented assumptions).

### 6.3 Dynamic Recommendations
- Synthetic: Recommends transitioning/adopting organic nutrient sources.
- Organic: Commended with positive finding; redundant transition recommendation suppressed.
- Integrated: Commended for balanced nutrient management.
- Missing: Recommends documenting baseline fertilizer records.
